About · since August 2023

A community of practitioners, run by volunteers

Cloud Native Security Pakistan (CNSPK) is a not-for-profit technical community for engineers and students working on cloud-native security, open source, Kubernetes, DevSecOps, and AI security.

01Mission

Why CNSPK exists

We make cloud-native security accessible to every Pakistani engineer, and Pakistani engineers visible to the global cloud-native ecosystem.

In practice that means free events on university campuses and in community spaces, hackathons that end in public repositories, and a stage for engineers who have real work to show. Since 2024, CNSPK has organised or co-organised 20 documented events in Islamabad, Lahore, Faisalabad, Quetta.

Relationship with CNCF

Listed as a community group on CNCF Open Community Groups, the platform the Cloud Native Computing Foundation runs for local communities. A listing on CNCF Open Community Groups is not an endorsement by CNCF of CNSPK's content or activities. See the listing (opens in a new tab).

03Values

How we work

  1. 01

    Rigor

    What is true matters more than what sounds good. We cite sources and say “we don’t know” when we don’t.

  2. 02

    Belonging

    No gatekeeping on credentials, English fluency, or city. The first ten minutes of a meetup are for the newest person in the room.

  3. 03

    Bridge

    We connect Pakistani engineers to global open source and CNCF projects, in both directions.

  4. 04

    Practice

    Every concept gets a demo, a code block, or a runnable example.

  5. 05

    Stewardship

    The community outlasts any one organiser. We document, rotate roles, and refuse vendor capture.

04Structure

Structure and governance

CNSPK is volunteer-run and not-for-profit. Events are free to attend. Organisers plan events, find venues and sponsors, and review talk proposals; volunteers help run the day.

Sponsors and collaborators are credited with their exact role on each event page, and talk selection excludes sales pitches.

No written governance charter or annual report has been published yet. Questions about how decisions are made can go to hi@cloudnativesecurity.pk. Conduct concerns go to conduct@cloudnativesecurity.pk.
