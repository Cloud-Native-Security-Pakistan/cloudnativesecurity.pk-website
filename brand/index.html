Brand

Brand assets and guidelines

Logos, colours, and type for event posters, slides, and partner materials. Use the files as supplied.

01Logo

Approved logo files

  • Stacked logo, dark background

    Posters, slides, and square social images.

    Download PNG

  • Horizontal lockup, white

    Headers and banners on dark backgrounds.

    Download SVG

  • Shield mark, transparent

    Watermarks, slide corners, and small placements on dark backgrounds.

    Download PNG

  • Avatar, shield on black

    Profile pictures on every platform.

    Download PNG

A dark-on-light version for printed and white backgrounds is not available yet. Ask hi@cloudnativesecurity.pk before recolouring the logo.

Do

  • Keep clear space of half the shield’s height on every side.
  • Use the white logo on dark or photographic backgrounds.
  • Write the name as “Cloud Native Security Pakistan” or “CNSPK”.
  • Place the CNCF logo beside ours, separated by a thin rule, when co-branding.

Don’t

  • Stretch, rotate, or recolour the shield.
  • Add shadows, glows, or outlines.
  • Use “CNSP” or “CNCF Pakistan”.
  • Merge the CNSPK and CNCF marks into one, or imply CNCF endorsement.

02Colour

Colour

Dark surfaces carry the brand. Electric lime is an accent on dark backgrounds only; on light backgrounds use Lime 600 for contrast.

  • Carbon#0F1115Page background
  • Charcoal#1A1D24Raised surfaces
  • Slate#2A2E37Rules and borders
  • Bone#F4F1EAPrimary text on dark
  • Muted#A3A8B3Secondary text on dark
  • Electric lime#C7FF3EAccent on dark only
  • Lime 600#9BD11AAccent on light surfaces
  • Pakistan green#01411CHeritage, used sparingly
  • Gold#C9A227Hairlines and the Urdu name

04Voice

How we write

  • Lead with what happened or what to do: “Run this command”, not “We’d like to suggest”.
  • Give the number, the date, and the source. Never round up or estimate in public.
  • Write for a global reader. Use one Roman Urdu word only where it adds warmth, never for technical terms.
  • CNCF is a credential, not our headline. CNSPK is its own community.
