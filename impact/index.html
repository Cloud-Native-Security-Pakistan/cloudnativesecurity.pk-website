08Method

How we measure impact

What counts as a CNSPK event

Headline event counts include only events organised or co-organised by CNSPK (20 of 21 records). Community partnerships and talks given by CNSPK members at other organisations’ events are listed separately and not counted.

Definitions

  • Attendance: people present at the event. “Verified” requires a public source; organiser-reported figures and estimates are labelled as such.
  • Registrations: sign-ups on a registration form. Not everyone who registers attends.
  • Capacity: places offered on a listing. Not attendance.
  • CNCF group members: registered members of the CNSPK group on CNCF Open Community Groups. Not unique event participants.
  • Project submissions: entries recorded by the hackathon platform (MLH).

The ~200 average

Community events have welcomed approximately 200 participants on average, based on organiser estimates. Historical attendance records are being consolidated. It is an organisation-level estimate supplied by the founder. It is not used as a figure for any single event and is not multiplied by the number of events to claim a lifetime reach.

Not measured yet

  • Unique participants across events.
  • Social audience sizes on WhatsApp, Discord, LinkedIn, and Instagram.
  • Speakers for 14 events and photos for 18 events.
  • Annual reports: none published yet.
