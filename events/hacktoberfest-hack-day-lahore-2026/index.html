Hacktoberfest Hack Day Lahore 2026

A one-day Hacktoberfest hackathon in Lahore, organised by CNSPK and listed by Major League Hacking. Teams built open-source AI projects and submitted them to MLH prize categories.

About this event

A one-day build event run by CNSPK as part of Hacktoberfest 2026, listed on Major League Hacking under the theme “AI for Everyone”. Students and developers worked in teams on open-source and open-weight AI projects, and submissions were judged in two MLH prize categories: Best Open-Source AI Project (DigitalOcean) and Best Use of Gemma 4 (Google DeepMind).

  • Open Source
  • AI
  • Hacktoberfest
  • Cloud Native
  • DevSecOps
  • MLH lists submissions closing at 6:45 pm; the organisers report event hours of 10 am to 6 pm.
  • The MLH submission gallery is not publicly viewable, so winners are organiser-reported.
