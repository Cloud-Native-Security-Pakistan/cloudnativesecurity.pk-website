Open Source Connect Pakistan

A community conference for open-source contributors, maintainers, and students, hosted by CNSPK at Systems Limited, Lahore, with GitHub as Gold sponsor.

About this event

CNSPK's largest gathering of 2026: an afternoon of talks and discussion on open-source contribution, maintainership, cloud-native projects, and developer tooling. The call for speakers ran on Sessionize from 25 to 27 August 2026 and explicitly excluded sales pitches. GitHub sponsored the event at Gold level.
