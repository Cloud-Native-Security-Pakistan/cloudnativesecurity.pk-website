02Formats

Ways to partner

Each format has happened at least once. The example shows where.

  1. 01

    Event sponsorship

    Support a meetup or conference: venue costs, refreshments, swag, or travel for speakers from other cities.
    GitHub sponsored Open Source Connect Pakistan 2026 at Gold level.

  2. 02

    Venue hosting

    Host an event at your campus or office. Most CNSPK events run on university campuses.
    5 university campuses have hosted events so far.

  3. 03

    Co-run a session

    Run a joint meetup with your community or bring CNSPK into your conference programme.
    Examples: AWS Community Pakistan, AWS User Group Faisalabad, ITCN Asia.

  4. 04

    Hackathon challenges

    Offer a challenge category or prizes judged on open-source work.
    Hack Day Lahore 2026 had categories from DigitalOcean and Google DeepMind via MLH.

  5. 05

    Workshop content

    Bring hands-on material and engineers who can teach it. No product pitches.
    Example: GitHub Copilot Dev Days Lahore, May 2026.

03Ground rules

How partnerships work

  • Each partner is credited with its exact role (sponsor, collaborator, venue host) on the event page, with a link to the public listing.
  • Talks are selected on merit. Calls for speakers exclude sessions that mainly promote a product or service.
  • Events stay free to attend.
  • Community work is separate from commercial services. If you need paid consulting, the consultants directory introduces members directly; it is not part of any sponsorship.
